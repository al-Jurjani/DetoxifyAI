# Category-Based Rephrasing Examples

## Category: Personal Insults

### Competence Attacks
| Toxic | Professional | Key Transformation |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|
| "You're an idiot" | "I think there's been a misunderstanding" | Attack → Neutral observation |
| "Are you stupid?" | "Could you explain your reasoning?" | Insult → Genuine inquiry |
| "What a moron" | "This concept can be tricky" | Label → Empathy |
| "You're completely useless" | "Let's identify where you need support" | Absolute → Specific support |

### Work Quality Attacks
| Toxic | Professional | Key Transformation |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|
| "This is garbage" | "This doesn't meet our quality standards" | Emotional → Objective |
| "Amateur hour" | "This seems less polished than usual" | Mockery → Observation |
| "A child could do better" | "This needs significant improvement" | Comparison → Standard |
| "Worst work I've ever seen" | "This requires substantial revision" | Hyperbole → Specific |

## Category: Threats & Aggression

### Retaliation Threats
| Toxic | Professional | Key Transformation |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|

| "I'll ruin you" | "I'll need to escalate this" | Personal attack → Process |
| "Watch your back" | "I have concerns about this approach" | Intimidation → Direct concern |
| "You'll pay for this" | "We need to establish accountability" | Revenge → Professional consequence |

### Aggressive Demands
| Toxic | Professional | Key Transformation |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|

| "I don't care about your excuses" | "Despite challenges, this deadline is firm" | Dismissive → Acknowledging + firm |

## Key Transformation Techniques Summary

1. **Absolutes → Specifics**: "You never" → "I've noticed three times"
2. **Personal → Behavioral**: "You're incompetent" → "This work needs improvement"
3. **Emotional → Objective**: "This is garbage" → "This doesn't meet standards"
4. **Attacks → Concerns**: "You're stupid" → "I'm concerned about this approach"
5. **Commands → Requests**: "Do it now" → "This is urgent. Can you prioritize?"
6. **Sarcasm → Direct**: "Oh great idea" → "I have concerns about this approach"
7. **Dismissive → Acknowledging**: "Whatever" → "I understand, though I disagree"
8. **Threats → Consequences**: "You'll regret this" → "This may have consequences"
9. **Mockery → Constructive**: "Genius move" → "I think there was an oversight"
10. **Gossip → Direct**: "Did you hear about X?" → "I have concerns about X's performance"
